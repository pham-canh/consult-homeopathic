Vultr: Agent Rush · Challenge 2

See a doctor in minutes, not a lost afternoon.

An AI intake agent gathers the history, screens for emergencies, and prepares a structured case. A real clinician reviews it and decides. Information work is automated; judgment stays human.

What happens to your case

1
Conversational intakeTell us the symptom, how long, allergies, medicines, and an email.
2
Emergency gateA deterministic red-flag check stops the flow and tells you to seek emergency care.
3
Care optionsTwo or three paths with plain-language reasons; you choose one.
4
Doctor reviewA clinician edits and approves the plan on the dashboard.
5
DeliveryThe approved plan is emailed to you, with a follow-up check.

The server can use Vultr, OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint; keys stay server-side. The app and database run on a Vultr VM. Patients can attach a lab report (.txt, .md, .csv, .pdf). No real patient data is used.
